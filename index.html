The person behind the ads.

Hi — I'm Ken Pilapil, CEO of OmniGrowth Marketing Co., a paid media and automation agency based in the Philippines. I work with coaches, agencies, and service businesses who are tired of spending money on ads that don't deliver booked calls.

My approach is simple: understand your offer deeply, build a funnel that matches how your clients think, run ads that attract the right people — then let automation do the follow-up so nothing falls through the cracks.
